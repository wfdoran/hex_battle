# new feature: "anchored"

If a unit did not more last turn and did not more this turn during the movement
phase, then it is "anchored".  Anchored units get +1 in their attack.  This
only applies for ranged attacked.  It goes away if they move.

Display their attack as x+1 where x is their normal attack.
